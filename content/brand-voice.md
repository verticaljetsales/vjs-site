This is the single source of truth every content agent reads before writing a

sellers on every side of the deal and back every recommendation with real
market data.

## Voice — how VJS sounds

We sound like the most trusted person in the room, not the loudest.

| We ARE | We are NOT |
|---|---|
| Confident, calm, precise | Hypey, salesy, "🔥 DEAL ALERT 🔥" |
| Data-driven and specific | Vague or exaggerated |
| Discreet and professional | Gossipy or naming client details |
| Plain-spoken and clear | Jargon for its own sake |
| Genuinely expert | Try-hard or gimmicky |
| Quietly aspirational | Flashy or braggy |

**Read-aloud test:** if Ben wouldn't say it to a $10M buyer across a table,
it doesn't ship.

### Tone by platform
- **LinkedIn** — the flagship. Peer-to-peer expertise. Market insight, honest
  takes on value, "here's what most people get wrong about X." Authority first,
  aircraft second.
- **Instagram** — the aircraft is the hero. Cinematic, aspirational, tight.
  Let the jet do the talking; caption adds one sharp insight + a soft CTA.
- **Facebook** — same asset as Instagram, slightly warmer/longer, more
  willing to spell out the "why it matters." Reaches an older owner-operator
  audience and aviation groups.
- **TikTok / Reels** — energetic but never cringe. Hook in 2 seconds, one
  idea, plain English. "What $3M actually buys you in a light jet." Educate to
  earn the follow.

## Content pillars (what we're allowed to talk about)

1. **Inventory spotlights** — a specific aircraft we represent, framed around
   what makes it a smart position (fresh maintenance, program coverage, low
   time, value vs. the market).
2. **Market intelligence** — pricing trends, supply/demand, "know your position
   before you're asked to defend it." This is where authority is built.
3. **Buyer / seller education** — how a pre-buy works, what a spec sheet really
   tells you, when to sell, acquisition vs. brokerage. Answers real questions.
4. **Founder / team POV** — Ben and the team's honest perspective on the
   industry. Human, credible, first-person.
5. **Behind the deal** — the discreet, professional version of "how we work,"
   never breaching client confidentiality.

## Hard rules (never break)

- **Never invent specs, hours, pricing, or maintenance status.** Every factual
  claim about an aircraft must come from its data file. If a field is blank,
  don't fill it — write around it.
- **Never publish a dollar price** unless the listing's `price` is an actual
  number the client has approved for public marketing. Default to
  "Price on request" / "priced to the market."
- **Never name a client, seller, or buyer**, or reveal why an aircraft is on
  the market. Discretion is the product.
- **No guarantees or investment-return promises.** Aircraft are not described
  as appreciating assets or "investments."
- **No competitor bashing.** We win on trust, not trash talk.
- **Registration / tail numbers** are only used publicly if already public on
  the listing. When in doubt, refer to the aircraft by year + make/model.
- Every factual, market, or pricing claim is **draft-only** until a human
  approves it. Agents never mark their own work "approved."
