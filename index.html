Ben McPeak
President / CEO
ben@verticaljetsales.com

Ben draws from over two decades of experience in corporate aviation, overseeing transactions and consulting in both domestic and international markets. Prior to joining the world of aviation, Ben held the position of Senior Vice President for a leading Global Wealth Management Group within US Trust. In this role, he focused on providing comprehensive wealth management solutions to ultra-high net worth individuals and families, with an emphasis on lending, investment planning, legacy and philanthropy issues, as well as complex tax strategies.

He brings the same breadth of knowledge and comprehensive planning into each of his aircraft transactions. Ben is also an active member of NBAA, AOPA, and CJP. When he isn't working, Ben enjoys spending time with his wife and their two children.

Jay Vidlak
Executive Vice President – Sales
jay@verticaljetsales.com

Jay has been around aviation his entire life, with both his grandfather and father being avid recreational pilots. After graduating in 1998 from the University of Nebraska at Omaha, Jay began his professional career in Aviation with Mayo Aviation in Denver, Colorado, where he quickly moved through the ranks.

He remained in that role until 2023 when he transitioned to Vertical Jet Sales, where his depth of knowledge is indispensable. Jay resides in Woodstock, Georgia, with his wife, Erin, and two little girls, Piper and Logan.
