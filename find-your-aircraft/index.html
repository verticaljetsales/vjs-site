Figures are indicative planning numbers, not performance guarantees. Where a manufacturer publishes range at a stated load, that figure is used and shown with its source, converted to passengers for the pilot count selected (two pilots unless one is selected and the aircraft is certified single-pilot); otherwise range is a planning estimate between ferry range and the listed seats-full range. A 10% allowance for winds and routing is added, and aircraft within 15% of your trip are shown as borderline. Manufacturer takeoff figures replace listed field lengths where available. Listed field lengths come from AviaCost and are not defined on a consistent basis, so they flag rather than exclude aircraft. Operating cost covers fuel at $6.50 per gallon plus airframe and engine maintenance reserves only, not crew, hangar, insurance, or financing. Cost and range figures marked AviaCost come from VJS AviaCost studies; the rest are VJS estimates. Market values and for-sale counts come from JETNET and reflect the middle of each fleet; individual aircraft vary widely by year, hours, and equipment.
